# 英文版敏感性分析图：论文使用说明

建议放在论文的“敏感性分析”小节，排在实验条件说明之后。双栏论文宜跨双栏插入，以便读者辨认逐窗散点和热力图数值。排版优先选用 [英文矢量 PDF](Sensitivity_Analysis_EN.pdf)，需要位图时使用 [300 dpi PNG](Sensitivity_Analysis_EN.png)。

## 英文图注（可直接使用）

**Sensitivity to visible history, observation delay, and service time.** Top row (Q1): window-level normalized average displacement error (NADE) versus visible history length at forecast horizons of 0.2, 0.6, and 1.0 s. Middle row (Q2): window-level NADE versus observation return delay during a 1 s occlusion, with either full history or the most recent 0.30 s of history. Each split violin shows test windows on the left (red, *n* = 390) and development windows on the right (blue, *n* = 212). Each point represents one window. White dots mark arithmetic means, and white bars span the 25th to 75th percentiles. Lower NADE indicates greater accuracy. Bottom row (Q3): mean weighted on-time rate (%) as a function of the service time multiplier α and added service time β. The two batches use separate sets of 300 seeds each. Values are averaged equally across the three scheduling cases. Both heatmaps use the same color scale, with higher values indicating better performance.

## 正文如何读图

| 位置 | 比较方法 | 适合在正文中说明的结果 |
|---|---|---|
| 第一行 · Q1 | 在同一个预测时长面板内，固定颜色，从完整历史读到最近 0.30 秒。三个面板的纵轴范围各自设置，跨面板比较请读刻度。 | 在 1.0 秒预测下，历史截短使开发集和既有测试集的分层平均 NADE 分别上升 2.59% 和 2.74%。 |
| 第二行 · Q2 | 在同一个历史条件下，沿横轴从 100 读到 200 毫秒，观察误差分布随延迟的变化。 | 完整历史下，回传延迟翻倍使开发集和既有测试集的分层平均 NADE 分别上升 25.54% 和 28.01%。 |

**图与三线表的统计口径。** 前两问的白点按窗口等权计算算术均值，白线描述窗口间的离散程度。正文和三线表中的相对变化按记录、地点、数据来源逐层汇总后计算，应从分层平均 NADE 列取数。每侧小提琴的最大宽度单独归一化，样本量由图注中的 *n* 给出。[前两问分层汇总数据](trajectory/trajectory_cells.csv) · [第三问分批工况数据](schedule_cells.csv)

## 英文正文示例

At the 1.0 s forecast horizon, restricting the visible history to 0.30 s increased the hierarchically averaged NADE by 2.74% on the test windows. With full history available, increasing the observation return delay from 100 to 200 ms increased the same metric by 28.01% during a 1 s occlusion. For scheduling, increasing the service time multiplier from 1.0 to 1.5 at β = 0.5 s reduced the mean weighted on-time rate by 18.10 percentage points in Batch 1 and 17.93 percentage points in Batch 2. Across all 15 service time settings, the two batch estimates differed by at most 0.80 percentage points.

结论范围为已测轨迹窗口、三个调度案例及其经验工期池。两批热力图支持工期效应在更换种子后可以复现，长工期造成的按时率损失应一并报告。相对变化和百分点差均使用舍入前的实验值。[完整实验方法与数据](敏感性分析与尾部风险_补充实验方法与数据.md)
